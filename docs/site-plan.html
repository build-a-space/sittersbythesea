Structure

Three sites, one brand

The main site is the brand front door. It explains who Sitters by the Sea is and sends people to their city. Each city site is where people book, and it is built to rank for that city's searches.

An SEO tradeoff to know about. Google treats each subdomain as a mostly separate site, so the city sites don't automatically share the main site's ranking strength the way folders like /virginia-beach/ would. Subdomains still work well for local businesses when each one gets its own Google Business Profile, its own reviews and links, and strong cross-links. The plan below does all of that. The current city pages already rank, so their old URLs will 301-redirect to the new subdomains to keep that traffic.

What to keep and what to change

Keep

  • The blue brand family, so returning clients still recognize you
  • "Sit. Stay. Go. Play." as the tagline
  • Real photos of client dogs on real beaches. They beat any stock photo.
  • The four-step getting started process
  • Trust badges: NAPPS, PSI, Nextdoor Fave, bonded and insured
  • Client Login and app download links into Time To Pet

Change

  • Script headlines are hard to read, especially on phones. Use a clear, friendly headline face instead.
  • Grey italic review text is low contrast. Make reviews larger and easier to read, and show star ratings with the total count.
  • Virginia Beach and Charleston share one page today. Give each city its own site, reviews and phone number.
  • The red splat and seagull decorations compete with the content. Use one accent color with a clear job: book buttons.
  • No prices and no clear "what happens after I book". Add both.
  • "Get Started" appears four times. One specific action, "Book a meet & greet", is clearer.
Design direction

Coastal, bright and trustworthy

This keeps your blue, but deepens it into a harbor navy that reads as more established. The rest of the palette borrows from the two coastlines, where most pet sitting sites fall back on paw prints. Virginia Beach gets buoy orange and boardwalk sand. Charleston gets haint blue, the pale porch-ceiling blue the city is known for, and Rainbow Row pastels. A deep harbor navy ties both cities back to the main brand.

Search

SEO plan

Target searches

  • "dog walker Virginia Beach", "pet sitter Virginia Beach"
  • "dog walking Charleston SC", "pet sitter Mount Pleasant"
  • Neighborhood searches: "dog walker Sandbridge", "pet sitter Isle of Palms", "Folly Beach dog sitter"
  • Military searches: "pet sitter near NAS Oceana", "dog sitting while deployed Charleston"
  • Service searches: "overnight pet sitter", "in-home dog sitting"

Technical basics

  • Static HTML pages so they load fast and score well on Core Web Vitals
  • LocalBusiness schema per city with address, hours, service area and ratings
  • Service, FAQ and Review schema where it applies
  • Separate XML sitemap and robots.txt per subdomain
  • Unique titles, descriptions and Open Graph images on every page
  • 301 redirects from every old URL to its new home

Local signals

  • One Google Business Profile per city, each linked to its own subdomain
  • A local phone number for Charleston (843) if you have one
  • Matching name, address and phone on Yelp, Nextdoor, Rover and directories
  • A review request after every first booking

Content

  • Neighborhood pages written with real local detail: beach dog rules, parks, walking routes
  • A monthly blog post per city, such as "Is my dog allowed on the Virginia Beach boardwalk in summer?"
  • A PCS and deployment guide for military families
Build

Node.js setup

One codebase builds all three sites. Shared parts (header, footer, walk report card, service cards, booking form) live in one place, and each city has its own content files. A fix made once shows up on every site.

Astro on Node.js

Builds plain HTML pages with almost no JavaScript, which is ideal for SEO and speed. Runs on Node 20+.

One repo, three builds

SITE=main, SITE=vb and SITE=chs each build their own site from shared components.

Hosting

Vercel or Netlify, one project per subdomain, all from this repo. Free SSL and a global CDN.

Forms & booking

Book, Client Login and app buttons go straight into your Time To Pet portal. A small Node endpoint handles the general contact form.
